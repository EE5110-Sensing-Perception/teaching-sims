\begin{frame}{Take-aways}
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item Know your \textbf{frames}. Most IMU bugs are sign or convention bugs.
    \item An accelerometer measures \textbf{specific force}: it cannot tell tilt, bias and acceleration apart.
    \item Every error term has a \textbf{growth law}. Allan variance and the datasheet tell you which one dominates.
    \item \textbf{Calibrate} what is deterministic, \textbf{estimate online} what drifts, and \textbf{gate} what lies.
    \item Fusion = predict with the gyro/IMU, correct with an absolute reference. Check \textbf{observability}.
    \item Unaided inertial navigation always diverges. \textbf{Aiding} sets how far.
  \end{enumerate}
\end{frame}
